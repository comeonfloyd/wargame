Deployment

The front runs from Leszno down to Wrocław, as on your sketch. You command Red in the west. Black holds the east and the Oder crossings.

  • Drag your dots to position them. You have 4 reserves to add troops with + Inf and + Armor.
  • In battle, drag a unit (or a selected group) to order a move. Line or right-drag spreads selected units along a front you draw.
  • Units take ground around them. Cut enemy units off from their map edge and they wither.
  • Win by taking Kalisz, the Black HQ, or by destroying every Black division. Lose Zielona Góra and you lose. Towns you hold speed up your reserves.
